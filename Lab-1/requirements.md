# Requirements Specification

## Functional Requirements

| ID | Type | Description | Priority | Acceptance Criteria | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-001 | Functional | The system shall enable attending physicians to author and digitally sign structured prescriptions that are automatically compiled into downloadable PDF records. | High | **Pass:** A prescription PDF contains a valid cryptographic signature and a validated physician license. **Fail:** A prescription is generated without successful license or signature validation. | Provides a trustworthy, portable clinical record and satisfies the supplied core requirement. |
| FR-002 | Functional | The system shall allow a patient to browse and search attending physicians by specialty and view each physician's verified profile, license status, and available consultation slots. | High | **Pass:** A patient can filter doctors by specialty and only verified profiles display bookable slots. **Fail:** An unverified physician is shown as available for booking. | Lets patients identify a suitable, licensed physician before booking care. |
| FR-003 | Functional | The system shall allow an authenticated patient to select an available video-consultation slot and create a booking after confirming the appointment details. | High | **Pass:** Selecting an open slot creates one appointment and marks that slot unavailable to other patients. **Fail:** Two patients can successfully book the same slot. | Provides the central appointment-booking capability and prevents double booking. |
| FR-004 | Functional | The system shall generate a unique, access-controlled tele-consultation room link for a confirmed appointment and permit the patient and assigned physician to join it during the scheduled time window. | High | **Pass:** Only the booked patient and assigned physician can use the link within the permitted window. **Fail:** An unauthenticated or unrelated user can join the room. | Enables secure remote consultation while protecting patient privacy. |
| FR-005 | Functional | The system shall send booking confirmations and appointment updates to both the patient and the attending physician through the notification service. | Medium | **Pass:** Both parties receive the correct date, time, physician, and booking status after creation, cancellation, or rescheduling. **Fail:** Either party receives no update or receives incorrect appointment details. | Reduces missed consultations and keeps both stakeholders synchronized. |

## Non-Functional Requirements

| ID | Type | Description | Priority | Acceptance Criteria | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NFR-001 | Nonfunctional - Security & Privacy | The system shall encrypt video-session metadata and patient clinical notes in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256; tele-consultation links shall be access-controlled and time-limited. | High | **Pass:** Security testing confirms TLS 1.3 for supported connections, AES-256 encryption for stored sensitive data, and rejection of expired or unauthorized room-link requests. **Fail:** Sensitive data is transmitted or stored without the required protection. | Healthcare information is highly sensitive and must be protected from unauthorized disclosure. |
| NFR-002 | Nonfunctional - Performance & Availability | Under a simulated peak load of 500 concurrent active users, 95% of specialty-search and slot-booking requests shall complete within 2 seconds, and the portal shall maintain at least 99.5% monthly availability. | High | **Pass:** Load-test results show the 95th percentile response time at or below 2 seconds and monitored uptime at or above 99.5%. **Fail:** Either threshold is not achieved. | Patients need timely access to appointments, particularly when seeking medical advice. |
